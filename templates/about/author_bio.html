About the Author

Brendan Deery is an IT audit and internal controls leader specializing in SAP-centric environments, automated business process controls, and IT general controls (ITGCs).

Professional Background

As a Senior Manager in IT Internal Audit, Brendan brings deep expertise in designing, testing, and optimizing internal controls over financial reporting (ICFR) within complex ERP ecosystems—including SAP S/4HANA, ECC, and SAP GRC architectures.

His work focuses on bridging the gap between technical system configurations and rigorous Sarbanes-Oxley (SOX) compliance frameworks, helping organizations build scalable, automated, and defensible control environments.

Credentials & Core Competencies

  • Professional Certifications: CA/CPA, CISA, CISSP
  • Core Specializations: SAP Business Process Controls, SAP ITGCs, Segregation of Duties (SoD) risk analysis, GRC Access Controls, and Audit Data Analytics.
  • Audit Methodology: PCAOB compliance standards, ICFR framework design, and automated control validation.
