About These Frameworks

This site provides practical baselines—a core set of SAP Business Process Controls (SOX) and SAP IT General Controls (ITGC)—around which organizations can build and mature their SOX programs.

The resources here cover key SAP business processes— {% for process in processes %} {{ process.name }} {% if forloop.revcounter == 2 %} and {% elif not forloop.last %}, {% endif %} {% endfor %} —with mapped SOX controls, process narratives, implementation guidance, and test procedures, as well as core ITGC layers like access management and change control across SAP environments.

Each control ID, for example, P2P-001 - Vendor Master Data - Dual Control, links directly to a detail page featuring control descriptions, related risks, implementation guidance, and test procedures.

Process narratives are also available for each area, such as the Procure to Pay process narrative, alongside ITGC narratives organized by layer and category.

These baselines are intended to be starting points, not final solutions. They help organizations assess their current state, design custom controls, plan testing schedules, and scale their controls as their SAP environment evolves.

What's Included

SAP Business Process Controls

Baseline control matrices for key SAP modules (Procure to Pay, Order to Cash, Record to Report, Acquire to Retire, and Inventory). Each includes business risks, control objectives, and designs mapped to compliance requirements.

IT General Controls (ITGCs)

Core ITGC frameworks for SAP access management, change control, batch processing, and backup management across enterprise landscapes.

Control Mapping & Workflows

Interactive workflow dashboards showing SAP business process flows and how controls map to compliance requirements. Click any process node to filter and explore related controls and audit evidence.

How to Use These Baselines

These frameworks are starting points, not final solutions. Use them to:

  • Assess current state: Compare your existing controls against the baseline to identify design gaps.
  • Design controls: Adapt control parameters to your specific business processes, SAP configurations, and risk profiles.
  • Plan testing: Use the built-in control matrices to scope and execute your SOX testing programs efficiently.
  • Maintain controls: Build upon these frameworks as your SAP landscape changes and your compliance organization matures.
